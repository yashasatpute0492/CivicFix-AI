How CivicFix AI Works (System Transparency)

A transparent look at our deterministic heuristics, priority scoring formula, duplicate detection, and architecture for real AI integration.

1. Local Rules vs. "Black Box" AI

Philosophy

In civic management, citizens and municipal supervisors need clarity and auditability. Instead of hiding prioritization behind an opaque algorithm, CivicFix AI currently runs an explicit, modular rule-based heuristics pipeline.

Future AI Readiness: The codebase is organized with a dedicated CivicRulesEngine class. When ready for production, this engine can securely connect to external Large Language Models (e.g., Google Gemini or OpenAI) via backend webhooks without changing frontend rendering.

2. Priority Scoring Formula (0 to 100)

Formula
Score = UrgencyBase + SafetyKeywords + SensitiveLocation + CommunityEndorsements
Factor Weight Range Examples & Local Rules
Citizen Urgency Base +10 to +45 pts Low (+10), Medium (+20), High (+35), Critical (+45)
Safety & Hazard Keywords Up to +30 pts Keywords: danger, hazard, injury, accident, collapse, spark, sinkhole, burst pipe
Sensitive Zone Proximity Up to +20 pts Locations containing: school, hospital, playground, kindergarten, clinic, highway
Contextual Vulnerability Up to +15 pts Terms: dark, night, unlit, flooding, blocked, sewer
Community Endorsements +5 pts each Each upvote adds +5 pts (capped at +20 pts total) to prioritize widespread issues

3. Possible Duplicate Detection Logic

Deduplication

When a citizen types a complaint, the engine checks existing issues within the local database:

  • Text Tokenization: Cleans punctuation, removes stop-words (the, at, in, near, on), and tokenizes both title and description.
  • Jaccard Word Overlap: Computes the intersection over union of keyword tokens.
  • Location Proximity Check: Checks street names and landmarks for match or substring equality.
  • Composite Similarity Score: If composite similarity exceeds 45%, a duplicate warning is rendered with an option to upvote the existing report instead of cluttering city queues.

4. Category Classifier Keyword Matrix

Auto-Suggestion

The system evaluates keyword frequencies to auto-suggest categories:

🛣️ Roads: pothole, asphalt, crater, curb, sidewalk, pavement 💡 Lighting: streetlight, lamp, bulb, blackout, dark, unlit, wiring 🗑️ Waste: trash, garbage, dumpster, litter, debris, odor, rats 🚰 Water: leak, pipe, flood, burst, sewage, drainage, gutter ⚠️ Public Safety: exposed, hazard, fallen tree, fire, collapse, wire 🌳 Parks: playground, bench, swing, overgrown, park lawn
